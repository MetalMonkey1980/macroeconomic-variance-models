**# 🧠 QUOTATION RULES**



Metal Monkey quotations follow a few simple principles.



\### 1. Preserve the original thought.



When a sentence first appears and carries meaning, keep the original version.

It may later be refined, but the original should never disappear.



\### 2. Do not pretend borrowed words are ours.



Historical quotations must retain their real author and source.



If authorship is uncertain, it must be marked as uncertain.



\### 3. New Metal Monkey quotations are explicitly marked.



Original thoughts created during the project are attributed to:



> \*\*Metal Monkey Project\*\*



\### 4. A quotation does not need to be perfect.



It only needs to contain an idea worth keeping.



\### 5. Context matters.



Whenever possible, record where and why a quotation appeared.



A sentence without its story may lose part of its meaning.

**# 🐘 FINAL NOTE**



These quotations are not intended to describe the world

from one single perspective.



They are fragments.



Different places around the same elephant.



Some may contradict each other.



Some may eventually be replaced by better formulations.



Some may turn out to be wrong.



That is allowed.



Because Metal Monkey does not believe that finding a better perspective

requires destroying the one that came before.
