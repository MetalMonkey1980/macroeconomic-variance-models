> *"The test does not have to prove who you are. It only has to make you wonder why you chose what you chose."* (Quote #09)

*This test is not a verdict on your nature. It is a mirror held up to your current perspective. It will help you see where you are standing around the elephant of reality and what bridges connect you to others in the Council of 60. Choose what feels most like you. Do not judge what feels most like someone else.*

### ☯️ LAYER 1: Approach Polarity (The Bridge of Energy)
<!-- LAYER: yin_yang | DIMENSION: directness_vs_reflection -->
> *"A good question does not tell you what to think. It shows you what you naturally notice."* (Quote #18)

**When you try to understand a situation or solve a problem, which of the two strategies naturally attracts you the most?**

*   **A) Strategy of Action (Yang):** Rather than thinking about things for a long time, it is better to start acting immediately, go directly to the point, and assert one's will outwardly. <!-- VALUE: yang -->
*   **B) Strategy of Reflection (Yin):** Rather than acting blindly, it is better to withdraw into calm, quietly analyze the situation, and wait for the right inner impulse. <!-- VALUE: yin -->

### 🎡 LAYER 2: Internal Engine (The Bridge of Common Ground / Trigon)
<!-- LAYER: trigon | DIMENSION: exploration_vs_preservation -->
> *"A different answer is not necessarily a wrong answer. It may simply come from a different place around the elephant."* (Quote #07)

**Imagine you are standing before a new phase of life or work. Which of these four challenges internally attracts you the most and sparks your curiosity?**

*   **A) The Challenge of Discovery (The Innovator):** Finding hidden strategies, novel combinations, and clever solutions in places where others see only chaos or a dead end. <!-- VALUE: trigon_1 -->
*   **B) The Challenge of Depth (The Intellectual):** Drilling beneath the surface of things, gathering facts, analyzing data, and building a system on solid, unquestionable foundations. <!-- VALUE: trigon_2 -->
*   **C) The Challenge of Freedom (The Independent):** Opening up space for direct action, following pure instinct, experiencing absolute autonomy, and refusing to be bound by convention. <!-- VALUE: trigon_3 -->
*   **D) The Challenge of Harmony (The Diplomat):** Creating aesthetic values, building an environment of trust, sensing the emotions of others, and forging bridges of understanding where conflict threatens. <!-- VALUE: trigon_4 -->

**If you had the opportunity to instantly experience one of the following three scenarios, which "temptation" would be irresistible to you right now?**

*   **A) Secret:** "I have an exclusive, strictly guarded secret and access to information nobody else knows. Come with me behind the curtain, but not a word to anyone." <!-- VALUE: intersection_cardinal -->
*   **B) Spotlight:** "This is your chance to show yourself in full, attract everyone's attention, and make sure the world never forgets how exceptional you are." <!-- VALUE: intersection_mutable -->
*   **C) Challenge:** "Everyone says it is impossible and nobody can do it. Are you ready to take the risk, break the limits, and prove them wrong?" <!-- VALUE: intersection_earth -->
