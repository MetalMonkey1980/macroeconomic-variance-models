# 🌌 Council of 60 — Conceptual and Structural Map

This document defines the exact coordinate system of the Council of 60. Each of the 60 characters is located at the intersection of four independent layers: Approach Polarity (Yin/Yang), Internal Engine (Trigons), Life Role (Seasonal Intersections), and Space Cultivation (Elements).

---

## 1. ☯️ LAYER 1: Approach Polarity (Yin / Yang)

Determines the primary direction of energy flow and the initial strategy when interacting with reality.

*   **Yang (Active Approach):** Energy directed outward. Expansion, direct action, rapid decision-making, and projecting one's will onto the external world.
*   **Yin (Receptive Approach):** Energy directed inward. Internalization, quiet analysis, deep intuition, and waiting for the right inner impulse.

---

## 2. 🎡 LAYER 2: Internal Engine (The Four Trigons)

Divides the 12 animal archetypes into 4 relational triads based on how their minds process information and what constitutes their primary inner motivation.

### 🏛️ Trigon 1: The Innovators (Rat, Dragon, Monkey)
*   **Core Trait:** A mind constantly running ahead. Strategic thinking, sharp curiosity, and a drive to find novel combinations or clever solutions where others see a dead end.

### 🧠 Trigon 2: The Intellectuals (Ox, Snake, Rooster)
*   **Core Trait:** Pragmatism and depth. Making decisions based on facts and data, high internal organization, and a need to drill beneath the surface to build on solid ground.

### 🐅 Trigon 3: The Independent (Tiger, Horse, Dog)
*   **Core Trait:** A fierce desire for freedom and autonomy. Direct action guided by pure instinct, raw authenticity, high dynamism, and a need for open space to breathe.

### 🤝 Trigon 4: The Diplomats (Rabbit, Goat, Pig)
*   **Core Trait:** A pursuit of harmony and peace. High empathy, deep sensitivity to the emotions of others, aesthetic awareness, and a natural ability to build bridges and smooth over conflicts.

---

## 🏛️ LAYER 3: Life Role (The Three Seasonal Intersections)

This layer divides the 12 animals into 3 cross-cutting groups of 4 animals (exactly one from each trigon). It determines how an archetype naturally responds to time, movement, and life transitions.

### 🎯 Intersection A: Cardinal Signs / Peaks of Seasons
*   **Composition:** Rat (T1), Rooster (T2), Horse (T3), Rabbit (T4)
*   **Core Trait:** Natural leaders and trendsetters. Intensive processing, an inherent drive, and the capacity to withstand immense pressure while holding the steering wheel steady in the middle of a storm.

### 🎡 Intersection B: Mutable Signs / Initiators of Seasons
*   **Composition:** Monkey (T1), Snake (T2), Tiger (T3), Pig (T4)
*   **Core Trait:** Change-makers and transformers. They love movement, flexibility, travel, and starting new projects on a blank canvas. They feel completely at home navigating chaos and transition.

### 🧱 Intersection C: Earth Signs / Guardians of Seasons
*   **Composition:** Dragon (T1), Ox (T2), Dog (T3), Goat (T4)
*   **Core Trait:** Protectors of continuity and stabilizers. Resilient anchors who resist rushed or ungrounded changes. Their role is to give chaos a clear form, anchor moving parts, and safely see things through to completion.

---

## 🛠️ LAYER 4: Space Cultivation (The Five Elements)

Elements represent the unique optical lenses through which the archetypes view the world, as well as the specific tools they use to reshape their surroundings.

*   **🌲 Wood (Horizons & Growth):** Focusing on the future, long-term vision, continuous self-development, strategic planning, and creative expansion.
*   **🔥 Fire (Spark & Passion):** High dynamism, intense emotion, inspiration, and the ability to instantly ignite motivation in others, injecting courage into stagnant spaces.
*   **🧱 Earth (Foundations & Stability):** Reliability, fairness, practicality, anchoring physical reality, and building safe, sustainable environments for others.
*   **⚔️ Metal (Structure & Order):** Crisp logic, discipline, boundaries, an eye for detail, precision in execution, and uncompromising justice.
*   **💧 Water (Flow & Connection):** High adaptability, deep intuition, empathetic sensing of minor nuances, quiet human networking, and fluidly navigating past obstacles.
